## Sales Performance Dashboard 

An interactive Excel dashboard that analyzes sales performance across **time, regions, products, and payment methods**. The dashboard transforms transactional sales data into business KPIs and visual insights using PivotTables, PivotCharts, and slicers.

## Business Questions
This dashboard was designed to answer the following questions:<br>
- How is the business performing overall?<br>
- How does revenue change over time?<br>
- Which regions generate the highest revenue?<br>
- Which products contribute most to sales?<br>
- How does revenue vary across customer types and payment methods?<br>

## Key Performance Indicators
- Total Sales<br>
- Total Orders<br>
- Total Quantity Sold<br>
- Average Order Value<br>

## Revenue Analysis
The dashboard provides interactive analysis of revenue by:
- Year<br>
- Region<br>
- Product Category<br>
- Payment Method<br>
- Customer Type<br>

## Key Insights
- **Overall Performance:** Q1–Q3 revenue remained relatively stable in 2023 and 2024 but declined to $910.17K in Q1–Q3 2025, approximately 31% below Q1–Q3 2023.<br>
- **Time & Seasonality:** March recorded the highest-revenue month in 2023 and 2025, suggesting a recurring seasonal pattern in monthly sales performance.<br>
- **Regional Performance:** North generated the highest revenue among regions in the overall dataset.<br> 
- **Product performance:** Tablet generated the highest revenue among the product analyzed.<br> 
- **Purchasing behavior:** Retail and wholesale customers showed different payment patterns. Cash consistently accounted for around 23–25% of retail sales, while wholesale customers changed their most-used payment method over time—from online payments in 2023 (27%), to credit card in 2024 (24%), and cash in 2025 Q1–Q3 (31%).<br>

## Analysis Process
The dataset was provided in a clean format, allowing the analysis to focus on business metrics rather than data cleaning. PivotTables were used to aggregate transactional data and calculate key metrics such as total revenue, total orders, quantity sold, and average order value before building the interactive dashboard.
